Petalwave is an interactive generative music instrument that runs in your web browser. It creates evolving electronic soundscapes by combining 36 toggleable instruments arranged in a 6x6 grid — pads, arpeggios, bass lines, drum patterns, atmospheric textures, and melodic voices — all driven by a harmonic engine that handles chord progressions, key changes, and scale theory automatically. At its center is a living kaleidoscope visualizer whose flower-like petals pulse and dance in response to the audio frequencies; you can click and drag the petals to physically reshape the sound, pulling them outward to brighten filters and add energy, or letting them settle back for a more ambient feel. The Complexity knob controls how adventurous every layer becomes — from simple triads and steady patterns at low settings to extended chord voicings, surprise substitutions, ghost notes, and faster arpeggios at high settings — while the Energy slider governs overall intensity from whisper-quiet ambient drones to full-force euphoric drops. Choose from five mood presets (Euphoria, Deep, Psychedelic, Melancholy, and Ascension), adjust the musical key, scale, and tempo, then mix your own sonic garden by lighting up any combination of the 36 instruments in the grid.
